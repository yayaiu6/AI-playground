AI English Tutor

A real-time English tutor that listens to the learner, responds aloud, and teaches through spoken conversation. The interaction is designed around speaking and listening, rather than a text-chat lesson.

ReactPythonLLMLive product
Open live tutorContact Yahya

The learning interaction

Many digital language exercises rely on typing. This project focuses on a spoken exchange: the learner talks, the tutor listens and replies with audio, and practice continues as a conversation. The portfolio describes the experience as real time and voice based.

What the system does

  • Accepts spoken input from the learner.
  • Responds aloud as an AI English tutor.
  • Supports interactive spoken English practice.

My role and implementation

Yahya Mahroof presents the tutor as a project he built. The portfolio lists React, Python, and an LLM among its technologies. More detailed model, speech-service, and deployment architecture is not documented in the available project materials, so those details are intentionally omitted.

The project is available as a live web experience. No performance, learning-outcome, or accuracy metrics are claimed here.
